Glass Catalogue

Manage the glasses, the sample artwork and the lighting for the visualiser, then download glasses.json and upload it next to the visualiser.

No glasses yetDrop one or more exported .json files anywhere on this page, or use "Add glasses…".

The artwork customers see before they upload their own. Each one becomes a sample button in the visualiser, in this order; the one marked "Shown first" is on the glass when the page opens. The five built-in samples are listed here too and can be removed. Your own images are stored inside glasses.json, so there is nothing else to upload.

No sample artworkThe visualiser will open with the built-in Botanical design on the glass and no sample buttons. Add PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG files here; transparent PNGs print only the ink.

The lighting the glass reflects. Use the built-in studio, or a panorama of your own: in KeyShot's HDRI editor, save the environment as .hdr (2048 × 1024 is plenty). A .jpg or .png panorama also works, with flatter highlights.

Lighting files are not stored inside glasses.json. Upload each one to the website at the path shown on its card, for example a lighting folder beside index.html. To try one first, drop the .hdr onto the visualiser's 3D view.

The background color the visualiser opens with. Customers can still change it for themselves with the background button on the 3D view; its Reset returns to this color.
